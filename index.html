      Your first merge starts the sequence.

      A completed sequence gives an upper bound on twin-width.

      Try, undo, try again.

      Drop one vertex onto another to contract them. Hovering over a target previews the new edges and maximum red degree. Move a vertex into empty space to change the drawing.

      Scroll or pinch over the canvas to zoom; drag the background to pan. The − / + buttons zoom around the center, Fit brings the whole graph back into view, and Re-layout runs the force-directed layout immediately. With the canvas focused, use − / + / 0.

      For a subdivided clique, the subdivision count is the number of new vertices inserted into each original edge. A count of 0 leaves the clique unchanged.

      Live force layout keeps the force simulation running while the graph is unsettled. Turn it off to freeze the current positions; dragging and panning pause it temporarily and it resumes when you release.

      To keep a contraction preview on the canvas, select two vertices and double-click either one. You can also select the first vertex, then double-click the second directly. Choose Merge selected vertices when ready.

      Press Escape to cancel a drag or clear a selection.

      What happens to the edges?

      Two black edges to the same neighbor stay black. Two absent edges stay absent. Every other combination becomes red. Red edges stay red through further contractions.

      The number beside a vertex is its red degree. “Width so far” is the largest red degree seen in your sequence; Back restores that value too.
